Pier to Pier (5.4 mi) Pier to Rose (3.0 mi) Numbered landmarks

Landmarks on the way

  1. 1. Santa Monica Pier Start and finish. Home of the Ferris wheel and the sign marking the end of Route 66.
  2. 2. Original Muscle Beach Rings, ropes and bars on the sand, where the beach fitness scene began in the 1930s.
  3. 3. Crescent Bay Park A small bluff-top park. The beach is at its widest here.
  4. 4. Ocean Park The last stretch of Santa Monica before the path crosses into Venice.
  5. 5. Rose Avenue The Venice Boardwalk starts here. The short route turns around at this point.
  6. 6. Windward Plaza The centre of Venice Beach: the skatepark is on your right and the Venice sign hangs over Windward Avenue.
  7. 7. Muscle Beach Venice The outdoor weight pen that made Venice famous.
  8. 8. Venice Fishing Pier The turnaround. Touch the pier and head home with the mountains ahead of you.
