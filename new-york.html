    What to expect

    Do I need to sign up?

    No. Just turn up. Logging the run in the Nike Run Club app is welcome and never required.

    How fast is it?

    Every pace is welcome. A coach leads and two pacers run with the group, one at the back.

    What happens after?

    We spend 15 minutes stretching and having coffee at the meeting point.

    Can I try shoes?

    Yes. The Mile Test table is open after the run. Run in a pair for up to 30 days and return them worn for a full refund.

    Do my miles count?

    Miles you log count towards your next Mile Pass tier.

    Will there be photos?

    A photographer joins one run a month. Ask a pacer for an opt-out wristband if you'd rather not appear.
