Bridge to Bridge (5.3 mi) Bridge to Bridge 5K (3.1 mi) Numbered landmarks

Landmarks on the way

  1. 1. Tate Modern Start and finish. The old Bankside Power Station, now a free modern art museum. The crew meets on the riverside terrace.
  2. 2. London Eye The 135-metre observation wheel. The path narrows here, so run single file past the queue.
  3. 3. Westminster Bridge The first crossing. Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament are straight ahead as you run over.
  4. 4. Cleopatra's Needle An ancient Egyptian obelisk on Victoria Embankment, guarded by two bronze sphinxes.
  5. 5. Somerset House The long neoclassical river front above the Embankment. Roughly the halfway point of the 5K.
  6. 6. Millennium Bridge The 5K turns onto this footbridge and runs straight back to Tate Modern. St Paul's dome is right behind you.
  7. 7. Tower of London Nearly a thousand years old. The riverside walk runs between the fortress wall and the Thames.
  8. 8. Tower Bridge The far turn. Climb the steps, cross between the towers and drop down to the south bank.
  9. 9. HMS Belfast A Second World War cruiser moored on the south bank, now a museum ship.
  10. 10. Borough Market One of London's oldest food markets. The last mile starts here.
